Projects

One AI project I’m proudest of, and the web work around it.

General AI Assistant with dynamic RAG

A chatbot that talks normally with a language model, and switches to answering from your own documents the moment you upload one.

  • Accepts PDF, Word and text files, then extracts, chunks and embeds them into a vector database.
  • Retrieves the most relevant passages and gives them to the model for grounded answers.
  • Switches automatically between normal chat mode and RAG mode.
  • Includes a web interface for uploading files and chatting.
